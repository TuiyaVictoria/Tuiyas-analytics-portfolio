
## [Project 1:Superstore Sales & Profitability Analysis](https://github.com/TuiyaVictoria/revenue-insight-analysis)
 Project Feature: Superstore Retail Analytics
The Problem: The business had high sales volume but inconsistent profitability across different regions and product lines. The Solution: I developed a series of SQL queries to audit 9,000+ transactions, calculating profit margins and identifying "loss-leading" products. Key Achievement: Identified that discounts over 20% were the primary driver of net losses, providing a data-backed recommendation to revise the pricing strategy. Tech Stack: SQL (Aggregations, Joins, CTEs), Data Visualization, Markdown.
